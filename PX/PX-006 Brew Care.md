# 1. Purpose  
  
PX-006 Brew Care defines the care, cleaning, drying, periodic care, long-term vehicle storage, and pre-use inspection of the THE THIRD PLACE Coffee System.  
  
This document is not intended to maximize cleaning.  
  
Its purpose is to:  
  
- remove Coffee residue and Milk residue where required  
- protect materials and finishes  
- avoid unnecessary washing  
- avoid unnecessary cleaning tools  
- prevent deterioration during long-term vehicle storage  
- return the Coffee System to a ready state after each use  
- verify condition before the next use  
  
Routine Care, Periodic Care, Descaling, and Long-Term Vehicle Storage are treated as separate functions.

# 4. Care Philosophy  
  
THE THIRD PLACE Coffee System does not treat cleaning as an objective in itself.  
  
The principle is:  
  
> **Clean only what needs cleaning, using only what is necessary.**  
  
The basic sequence is:  
  
```text  
Remove  
↓  
Rinse / Wash when required  
↓  
Dry  
↓  
Inspect  
↓  
Store  
```  
  
Not every Equipment item should be washed with water.  
  
Care must distinguish between:  
  
- Coffee residue  
- Coffee oil  
- Milk residue  
- Water residue  
- Material-specific requirements  
- Electronic components  
- Wood  
- Leather  
- Glass  
- Stainless Steel  
- Coated surfaces  
  
---  
  
# 5. Minimal Care  
  
Cleaning must not create unnecessary Equipment.  
  
Existing Equipment, manufacturer-provided accessories, shared brushes, cloths, and campground water facilities should be used first.  
  
A dedicated care item should only be added when:  
  
1. an actual functional gap exists  
2. existing Equipment cannot perform the function  
3. the manufacturer requires it  
4. it materially protects Equipment or safety  
5. it does not unnecessarily increase Workflow  
  
---  
  
# 6. No Unnecessary Washing  
  
Water exposure is not automatically beneficial.  
  
Routine water washing should be avoided when it may:  
  
- damage Wood  
- damage Leather  
- affect finishes  
- introduce moisture into Electronics  
- create unnecessary drying requirements  
- shorten product life  
- complicate long-term storage  
  
Dry Care is therefore preferred whenever it is functionally sufficient.  
  
---  
  
# 7. Operating Environment  
  
THE THIRD PLACE Coffee System is used outdoors, primarily at high-standard campgrounds.  
  
The assumed campground provides:  
  
- water  
- washing facilities  
- drainage  
- sufficient space for Routine Care  
  
Therefore, the standard is:  
  
> **Complete required Care at the campground before closing the site.**  
  
Care should not normally be postponed until returning home.  
  
---  
  
# 8. Long-Term Vehicle Storage  
  
The Coffee System may remain in the vehicle for approximately one month between uses.  
  
This includes summer conditions and potentially high interior temperatures.  
  
The System must therefore enter vehicle storage in a condition that minimizes:  
  
- Milk residue  
- Coffee residue  
- excessive Coffee oil  
- standing water  
- trapped moisture  
- wet Wood  
- wet Leather  
- moisture trapped inside cases  
- wet cleaning cloths  
  
The governing rule is:  
  
> **No wet Equipment enters Long-Term Vehicle Storage.**

# 10. Routine Care Workflow  
  
Routine Care is performed after the Coffee System has completed its operating cycle.  
  
The standard order is:  
  
```text  
Coffee / Milk Residue Removal  
↓  
Required Washing / Rinsing  
↓  
Material-Specific Care  
↓  
Drying  
↓  
Inspection  
↓  
Storage  
```  
  
Routine Care must not introduce unnecessary disassembly.  
  
---  
  
# 11. Espresso Closure  
  
After the final Espresso extraction:  
  
1. Allow the 9Barista to cool.  
2. Release pressure according to the manufacturer's procedure.  
3. Remove the Portafilter.  
4. Remove the Puck.  
5. Remove the Basket where required.  
6. Remove the Basket Cap Pro.  
7. Remove the Puck Screen.  
8. Remove Coffee residue.  
9. Rinse Coffee-contact components.  
10. Remove excess water.  
11. Allow all components to dry completely.  
  
Routine Care does not require complete disassembly of the 9Barista.

```text

↓

↓
```  

## 12.2 Water Rinse  
  
Water rinsing is the standard Routine Care method for the Coffee-contact components.  
  
Routine Care does not require detergent.  
  
## 12.3 Soap  
  
Soap is not part of the normal 9Barista Routine Care process.  
  
Do not introduce unnecessary soap into the Boiler or internal water path.  
  
If soap has accidentally entered the system, rinse according to the manufacturer's guidance.  
  
## 12.4 Dishwasher  
  
Do not use a Dishwasher for the 9Barista unless a specific component is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as dishwasher-safe.  
  
The standard THE THIRD PLACE procedure is manual rinsing.

# 17. 9Barista Coffee Oil Care  
  
Routine rinsing and Coffee Oil removal are separate functions.

- remove Coffee grounds  
- rinse  
- dry  
  
## Periodic  
  
When Coffee Oil accumulation becomes apparent, perform additional Coffee-contact surface care.  
  
Potential targets:  
  
- Portafilter  
- Basket  
- Basket Cap  
- Puck Screen  
- Coffee-contact surfaces  
  
Do not use aggressive degreasing after every extraction as a default practice.  
  
---  
  
# 18. 9Barista Descaling  
  
Descaling is separate from Coffee residue and Coffee Oil Care.  
  
Purpose:  
  
> **Mineral / Limescale Removal**  
  
It must not be treated as ordinary cleaning.  
  
## 18.1 Light Descale  
  
Perform when Scale accumulation or Flow Restriction indicates a need.  
  
This is not Routine Care.  
  
## 18.2 Deep Descale  
  
9Barista's current guidance identifies Deep Descaling as periodic maintenance.  
  
The standard interval is approximately annual.  
  
Hard Water conditions may require a shorter interval, but this does not become a routine after-every-camp procedure.  
  
## 18.3 Descaler  
  
Use only a Descaler compatible with 9Barista's manufacturer guidance.  
  
Manufacturer-documented examples include:  
  
- Puly Descaler  
- Urnex Dezcal Liquid  
- Durgol Swiss Espresso Special Descaler  
  
Do not use an unidentified Descaler.  
  
Do not substitute a Descaler merely because it is marketed for another Espresso Machine.

# 44. Camp Closure Protocol  
  
Before leaving the campground:  
  
## Step 1 — Remove Residue  
  
Remove:  
  
- Coffee Grounds  
- Coffee residue  
- Milk  
- Beverage residue  
  
## Step 2 — Wash Required Items  
  
Wash only items that require washing.  
  
## Step 3 — Rinse  
  
Rinse all washed items thoroughly.  
  
## Step 4 — Material Care  
  
Inspect:  
  
- Wood  
- Leather  
- Glass  
- Coated surfaces  
- Gaskets  
  
## Step 5 — Dry  
  
Dry all liquid-contact Equipment completely.  
  
## Step 6 — Inspect  
  
Confirm:  
  
- no Milk residue  
- no Coffee residue  
- no excessive Coffee Oil  
- no standing Water  
- no trapped moisture  
- no abnormal odor  
  
## Step 7 — Store  
  
Only Equipment that is ready for Long-Term Vehicle Storage is loaded into the vehicle.

# 45. Long-Term Vehicle Storage Rule  
  
The Coffee System may remain inside the vehicle for approximately one month.  
  
Therefore:  
  
> **Camp Closure is the final opportunity to complete required Care.**  
  
Do not rely on returning home to finish Routine Care.  
  
---  
  
# 46. Dryness as a Completion Condition  
  
Cleaning is not complete simply because visible contamination has disappeared.  
  
Care is complete only when:  
  
1. residue is removed  
2. required washing is complete  
3. required rinsing is complete  
4. material-specific care is complete  
5. excess water is removed  
6. Equipment is completely dry  
7. Equipment is ready for storage  
  
Therefore:  
  
> **Cleaning Complete ≠ Washing Complete**  
  
and:  
  
> **Storage Ready = Clean + Dry + Inspected**

After Long-Term Vehicle Storage, inspect the Coffee System before the next use.  
  
## General  
  
- abnormal odor  
- visible contamination  
- unexpected moisture  
- mold  
- corrosion  
- surface damage  
  
## Coffee Equipment  
  
- Coffee Oil  
- Coffee residue  
- Grinder condition  
- Tool condition  
  
## Milk Equipment  
  
- Milk odor  
- Milk residue  
- Pitcher condition  
- FIKA12 condition  
- Steam Tip condition  
  
## Glass  
  
- cracks  
- chips  
- water residue  
  
## Wood / Leather  
  
- moisture  
- cracking  
- warping  
- surface deterioration  
  
If an abnormal condition is found, perform appropriate Care before use.

- Descaling  
- Grinder internal care  
- Gasket inspection  
- Wood care  
- Leather care  
- Finish inspection  
- Glass inspection  
  
Periodic Care is performed according to Equipment condition and manufacturer guidance.  
  
It is not automatically added to every Camp Closure.

# 49. Cleaning Agent Policy  
  
Dedicated Cleaning Agents are not automatically carried for every camping session.  
  
Use Cleaning Agents only when:  
  
- the manufacturer specifies them  
- a verified periodic procedure requires them  
- a clear functional need exists  
  
Do not apply generic Coffee Machine Cleaner to Equipment without confirming compatibility.  
  
For 9Barista, do not use:  
  
- unnecessary Soap  
- incompatible Descaler  
- unidentified Chemicals

# 65. Design Principle  
  
THE THIRD PLACE Coffee System does not create Luxury by increasing Cleaning.  
  
It maintains Luxury by returning the Equipment to the correct state with the minimum necessary intervention.  
  
The Coffee System should therefore:  
  
- use the correct method  
- at the correct frequency  
- at the correct location  
- with the minimum necessary objects  
- and return to a ready state for the next use  
  
Outdoor operation must not require unnecessary inconvenience.  
  
---  
  
# 66. Core Rule  
  
> **Minimal Care**    
> **Maximum Readiness**  
  
The Coffee System should require no more Care than necessary while remaining immediately ready for the next Coffee Workflow.  
  
---  
  
# 67. Final Operating Principle  
  
> **Clean what needs cleaning.**  
>  
> **Protect what should not be washed.**  
>  
> **Dry everything before long-term storage.**  
>  
> **Maintain only when maintenance is required.**  
>  
> **Never add a tool without a functional reason.**

# 68. Status of Unverified Information  
  
Where manufacturer documentation is unavailable, information within PX-006 must be classified according to its evidence level.  
  
## Evidence Levels  
  
### Confirmed  
  
Directly confirmed by current manufacturer documentation or an authoritative project source.  
  
### Manufacturer Specified  
  
Explicitly stated by the manufacturer as a required or recommended procedure.  
  
### Verified Secondary Information  
  
Supported by reliable secondary documentation where manufacturer documentation is incomplete or unavailable.  
  
### User Experience  
  
Observed or reported by users, but not sufficient to establish an official procedure.  
  
### Unconfirmed  
  
Information that has not been sufficiently verified.  
  
Unconfirmed information must never be represented as manufacturer instruction.

# 70. Final Standard  
  
THE THIRD PLACE Brew Care is governed by the following sequence:  
  
```text  
USE  
↓  
REMOVE RESIDUE  
↓  
WASH / RINSE ONLY WHERE REQUIRED  
↓  
MATERIAL-SPECIFIC CARE  
↓  
COMPLETE DRYING  
↓  
INSPECTION  
↓  
LONG-TERM VEHICLE STORAGE  
↓  
PRE-USE INSPECTION  
↓  
READY  
```  
  
The desired state is not maximum cleanliness.  
  
The desired state is:  
  
> **A Coffee System that is clean where necessary, protected where necessary, dry before storage, and ready for the next use.**
