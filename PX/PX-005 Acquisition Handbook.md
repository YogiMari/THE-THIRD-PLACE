# Purpose  
  
PX-005 Acquisition Handbookは、PX-004 Barista Codexで正式採用されたCoffee Equipmentの購入情報を管理する正式文書である。  
  
本書では以下を管理する。  
  
- Official Purchase Source  
- Purchase URL  
- Japan Shipping  
- Warranty  
- Estimated Cost  
- Shipping Cost  
- Import Tax  
- Purchase Checklist  
- Purchase Notes  
  
Equipmentの採用理由・Workflow・Selection CriteriaはPX-004で管理する。

# Purchase Policy  
  
All purchases shall follow these principles.  
  
## Preferred Sources  
  
1. Official Manufacturer Store  
2. Authorized Distributor  
3. Domestic Authorized Retailer  
4. Amazon Japan (Official Seller)  
  
---  
  
## Purchasing Priority  
  
1. Genuine Product  
2. Latest Model  
3. Official Warranty  
4. Long-term Availability  
5. Combined Shipping (when possible)  
  
---  
  
## Import Guidelines  
  
For overseas purchases:  
  
- Budget for import tax and consumption tax.  
- Prefer consolidated shipping when ordering multiple items from the same manufacturer.  
- Retain invoices and customs documentation.  
- Register warranties whenever available.  
  
---  
  
## Maintenance Records  
  
For each purchased product, record:  
  
- Purchase Date  
- Seller  
- Order Number  
- Warranty Expiration  
- Serial Number (if applicable)

PX-005 Acquisition Handbook manages purchasing information only.  
  
Equipment selection, decision rationale, workflow, and adoption status are maintained exclusively in PX-004 Barista Codex.  
  
TP-004 Equipment Registry Object Reference references PX-004 for official equipment decisions and PX-005 for procurement information.
